How a teardown goes

  1. Pick something people use

    An app on your phone, a checkout you've been through, a feature you've noticed without noticing.

  2. Ask why about every choice

    Why that button, that order, that word? Every choice on a screen cost somebody a decision.

  3. Say what's seen and what's guessed

    I can't see their data, so I mark my guesses as guesses and say what I'd test to find out.

What should I break down next?

Send me a product you use every day, or one you can't stop wondering about. The best teardowns start with someone else's question.
