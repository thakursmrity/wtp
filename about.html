What this is

Why That Product is a place to learn how products get made by looking closely at ones you already know. I make short videos and written notes in four flavors: teardowns, quick verdicts on whether people would pay, diaries from my user conversations, and interview practice.

I don't pretend to have the inside story. I look at what's on the screen, make an honest guess about the decision behind it, and say what I'd test to find out if I'm right.

Who it's for

  • People who are curious how apps they use every day got to be the way they are.
  • People preparing for product interviews who are tired of memorizing frameworks.
  • Builders and founders who know they should talk to users and don't know where to begin.
  • Anyone who has ever stared at a screen and wondered why that button is there.

Ground rules

  • I say what I see first, then I say what I'm guessing. They never get blended.
  • I stay kind to the people who built it. They were working with constraints I can't see.
  • If I get something wrong, I say so in the next video and show what changed my mind.
  • No fake authority. I'm figuring this out in public, and you're welcome to argue with me.

What this isn't

It isn't advice from a famous product leader, and it isn't a polished course. Deeper walkthroughs may come later, and what you tell me now decides what they cover. If you're stuck on something, say so on the connect page.

A bit about me

I'm Smrity, an artist and dreamer at heart who got hooked on how products get made. I sketch, I build small things, and I ask a lot of questions. Here, I turn that habit into something you can watch and learn from. You'll see me get things wrong, and that's on purpose.
